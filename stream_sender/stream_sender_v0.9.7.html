SilVRCine // Stream Sender

The PC waits here with a fixed code. The cinema on the Quest connects to it, then this page shares the screen back over WebRTC. Run it inside the SilVRCine Bridge to start sharing automatically and to get remote mouse and keyboard from the Quest.

How to
  • With the Bridge (recommended): run run_bridge.bat on the PC once. It opens this page, waits for the Quest, shares the screen and injects the Quest's mouse and keys.
  • Without the Bridge: keep this page open. When the Quest connects, click Share and pick what to send. Video only.
  • On the Quest (Light Spill Lab v0.7.0+): type this code, press Connect, keep Auto on. Next time it connects by itself.
  • Audio: a browser tab share includes its audio. For the whole screen, tick Share system audio, or pick a recording device in the Bridge Settings tab (e.g. VoiceMeeter).
  • Apps: add your players in Settings (Bridge only). The Quest asks the Bridge for this list, so you pick which one to open from the headset.
Settings are saved by the SilVRCine Bridge. Open this page with run_bridge.bat to edit them.
Stream
PC speakers while the Quest is linked

Tick the outputs that are your speakers. The Quest keeps its sound (it comes from a virtual bus like B2 / Aux). They come back when the Quest disconnects or the Bridge closes.

VoiceMeeter was not found (or is not running).

Apps the Quest can open

Empty exe = auto detect (Stremio, PotPlayer, VLC). The profile sets the Quest buttons for that app.

Media library (browse and play from VR)

Point each category to one or more folders. On the Quest open Library in the menu (or hold Y), pick a video and it plays on the PC with the chosen player.

Artwork (posters and backdrops, like Kodi)

Works out of the box with the built-in key. Optional: your own TMDB API key. Optional fanart.tv key for richer backdrops. Names like Movie.Name.2017.mkv and Show.S01E02.mkv match best.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
