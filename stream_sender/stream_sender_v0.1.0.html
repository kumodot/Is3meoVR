Is3meo // Stream Sender

Run this page on your PC. It captures a browser tab, window or screen and streams it over WebRTC to the cinema on the Quest, where it plays on the big screen with light spill.

Idle
How to
  • On the Quest: open the Light Spill Lab (v0.5.0+), press Receive stream and note the 4 digit code.
  • Here: type the code, press Choose & send and pick what to share.
  • Audio: sharing a Chrome tab (e.g. web.stremio.com) sends its audio. For a desktop app window, share the Entire screen and tick Share system audio.
  • Quest and PC should be on the same network. Keep this tab open while streaming.
