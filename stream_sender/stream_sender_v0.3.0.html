The PC waits here with a fixed code. The cinema on the Quest connects to it, then this page shares the screen back over WebRTC. Run it inside the Is3meo Bridge to start sharing automatically and to get remote mouse and keyboard from the Quest.

How to
  • With the Bridge (recommended): run run_bridge.bat on the PC once. It opens this page, waits for the Quest, shares the screen and injects the Quest's mouse and keys.
  • Without the Bridge: keep this page open. When the Quest connects, click Share and pick what to send. Video only.
  • On the Quest (Light Spill Lab v0.7.0+): type this code, press Connect, keep Auto on. Next time it connects by itself.
  • Audio: a browser tab share includes its audio. For the whole screen, tick Share system audio, or let the Bridge pick a recording device (audio_device in its config, e.g. VoiceMeeter).
